# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

One Flutter app on Android and iOS with a single shared brand design, not a per-OS design language.

## Users

People paid by the week, fortnight, month or irregularly, anywhere in the world (ten languages, no market ranked first), who want to know what they can spend today without breaking what must be paid. They record money by hand; there is no bank connection.

## Product Purpose

Upino is a personal financial decision-support system. Its core output is Safe-to-Spend: how much can be spent now while protecting upcoming obligations, essential living costs, irregular costs, debt requirements and goals. Success is a person who trusts that number enough to act on it.

## Positioning

A deterministic Financial Engine owns every monetary calculation; Safe-to-Spend is the residual of an explicit priority waterfall (P0–P9), not a budget. AI may explain and assist but never computes the figure. It is not a zero-based budgeting or category-envelope app.

## Operating Context

Manual-first: balances, spends, income and bills are entered by hand, often in seconds (Quick Expense targets about three seconds). The plan is computed on the device and works offline.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Engine invariants of Product Foundation v3.5 (Frozen G0) and the v3.6 ledger addendum are fixed; both the TypeScript and Dart engines are checked against the same fixture table.
- Income lifecycle EXPECTED / LATE / MISSED, hard-obligation protection, essential living allowance, card reserve, goals, mandatory funding gap and liquidity confidence are authoritative.
- Sign-in with Google, Apple and email (one screen for sign-in and sign-up) is planned; no account service exists yet (open decision: Firebase or Supabase).
- Ten languages including RTL (Arabic, Persian); Jalali calendar in Persian.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Upino. Mark: rounded-square "U".
- Blue #2F3AE8 is the brand colour. Lime #CDFE6C is a second colour reserved for good money news (payday, money in, a goal met), only as a fill or on dark.
- Geist typeface; Solar line icons. Dark mode is first-class.
- Voice: plain, calm, factual; no jargon, no verdicts on purchases, no alarm styling for an incomplete profile.

## Evidence on Hand

No real users, testimonials, metrics or press exist. Sample plans are demo data only and must not be presented as real.

## Product Principles

1. Minimum input, first trustworthy number: value before a complete profile.
2. Every figure comes from the engine; no parallel or simplified calculation anywhere.
3. Say what is known and how sure it is; never pretend accuracy.
4. The reward for any effort is a useful decision, not a completion badge.
5. Nothing is recorded without the person's tap.
